Why your voice matters

Every answer helps us see what is working well and what needs to improve for workers.

Your experience counts

You know your daily work and living conditions best. Your honest feedback gives us the real picture.

Helps improve welfare

Your answers help us find where facilities and services can be made better for workers.

Simple to answer

Most questions only need you to tap one option, from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.

What the survey asks about

You will be asked how satisfied you are with the areas that affect your everyday life as a worker.

Your employment

Your overall satisfaction with working for the company.

Accommodation

Your accommodation and living conditions.

Food and catering

The food and catering arrangements provided to you.

Transportation

The transportation arrangements provided by the company.

The survey also includes more questions about the facilities and services provided to you.

How to complete the survey

Three simple steps.

1

Tap Start Survey

The survey opens on this page, so you can complete it without leaving the website.

2

Choose your answers

Pick the option that best matches your experience. Some questions also have a Not Applicable option.

3

Submit at the end

Go through all the sections and submit your answers when you finish.

Good to know: Questions marked with a red asterisk (*) are required. Please answer them before you submit.
